Canvas features

This list comes from demo/canvas-features.js. Add or update an entry there when the canvas chart gains a feature.

    How to read the numbers

    • Time to screen: from calling setChartProperties() until the browser has painted the next frame. It includes waiting for that frame (up to about 16 ms on a 60 Hz screen), so small differences at 1,000 points are mostly noise.
    • Script + parse: building the chart and the browser reading its markup, before painting.
    • Elements in chart: everything inside the chart's shadow DOM. SVG adds one <circle> per point; the canvas version adds one <canvas> no matter how many points.
    • Redraw average: re-renders the same chart 20 times, the way zooming or panning would.
    • Timings depend on your device and browser, and vary a little run to run. Click Render both to measure again.
